Production Supervisors

Bangalore, India Production Supervisors Diploma/B.E/B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering

About the Role

Leading the production shop floor Operators & Setters to assure the safe, qualitative and quantitative production targets including Zero Incident, assuring Zero Defects, OTD >98 % per agreed time to time production plan(s) and schedules, 100% Inventory accounting and reconcilation.

Job Description

  • Assure Achieving Qualitative & Quantitative production per Shift / Daily Production Plan targets
  • Ensure and report WIP Inventory Quality and Accuracy (Perpetual and Physical Count Cycle)
  • Allocation of Operators & Setters considering the availability & Skill level
  • Assure Setters and Operators Efficiency Monitoring & recording data
  • Conformance to Production Work Order On Time Delivery as per Original Promise
  • Ensure Customer Order On Time Delivery as per Original Promise
  • Assuring to Meet Agreed OEE Target through Monitoring, Initiating actions and follow-up and Reporting the impacts
  • Work together with Interfunctionals Viz., Engineering, Quality, Tooling & Maintenance to identify Production Risk and Mitigate
  • Assuring Engineering Documents and Records Availability
  • Drive Kaizen (Safety, People, Skill, Talent, Process, Quality, Setup Change, Tool Cost and etc.)
  • Following, Driving, ensuring Quality Management System and Customer Specific Compliances
  • Agile Reporting and communication of Performance, Progress, Escalations to Superior / Management

Key Result Areas

  • Production Plan vs Actual >98% – 45%
  • Achieve 100% OEE for production machines – 25%
  • Rejection/Rework reduction and control – 20%
  • Zero customer complaints – 10%

Key Skills

  • Technical Knowledge: High-mix, low-volume machining processes
  • Experience: 4–8 years
  • Personal Attributes: Communication, Leadership, Problem Solving, Critical Thinking, Organization
  • Domain Skills: CNC Turning, Milling, Grinding

Key Interactions

Internal:
  • Team Member, Quality: DIR, action implementation, and follow-up
  • Manufacturing Lead: OEE improvement actions and follow-up
  • Team Member, Engineering: SOPs, tools, gauges, instruments
  • Team Member, Maintenance: Incident resolutions and corrective actions
External:
  • Machine Supplier: Specifications, installation, repair
  • Tool Supplier: CTQ issues, CPC improvements
