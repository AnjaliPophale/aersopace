Capabilities

Crafting the Future of Flight—With Every Detail Engineered

Precision Machining

At JK Maini, we bring over two decades of expertise in high-precision aerospace machining. With a fleet of 180+ state-of-the-art CNC machines and the capability to handle 1,200+ unique SKUs, we deliver complex, tight-tolerance components that power modern aviation.

  • Turbine and compressor parts
  • Engine casings and structural components
  • Prismatic and turning operations across aluminum, titanium, Inconel, and stainless steel
  • 3-axis to 5-axis machining for high-complexity geometries
  • 100% traceability and in-process inspection

NDT & Welding

We are NADCAP-accredited for both Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) and specialized aerospace welding, ensuring compliance with the most demanding global aerospace standards.

NDT Capabilities:
  • Fluorescent Penetrant Inspection (FPI) – Level 2 & 3 certified technicians
  • Magnetic Particle Inspection (MPI) – For ferromagnetic materials and weld integrity
Welding Expertise:
  • TIG Welding (GTAW) for high-strength, precision-critical joints
  • Resistance Spot Welding (RSW) for sheet metal components
  • Welders certified to AWS D17.1 and other aerospace standards

Mechanical Assembly

We specialize in aerospace mechanical sub-assemblies, built with precision, durability, and compliance in mind. Our facilities support:

  • Riveting operations (solid, blind, Hi-Lok, and more)
  • Bush and Helicoil insertions using aerospace-grade fixtures
  • Sealing operations with qualified personnel and certified materials
  • Hydraulic pressing with programmable force and depth controls

Each assembly follows strict process validation and FAI protocols. Our team ensures consistency from prototype to production scale.

Metrology & Tooling

Accuracy is the cornerstone of aerospace manufacturing. Our dedicated metrology lab ensures every part meets exact specifications using:

  • Gauge calibration systems traceable to international standards
  • High-zoom optical inspection systems (10x and above)
  • CMM (Coordinate Measuring Machine) with scanning capabilities
  • Custom jigs and tooling for complex inspection routines
  • SPC and GR&R analysis for statistical confidence
